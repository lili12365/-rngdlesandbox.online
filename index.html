CLUE SYSTEM

Three signals, one hidden number.

The game does not reveal digits individually. Instead, it gives compact numeric feedback: direction, proximity, and final confirmation. That keeps the puzzle quick while still rewarding careful interval narrowing.

  • Too low - the target is greater than your guess.
  • Too high - the target is smaller than your guess.
  • Close - your guess is within 1,000 of the answer.
  • Hit - exact match, puzzle complete.
