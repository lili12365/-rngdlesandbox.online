CLUE SYSTEM

Three signals, one hidden number.

The game does not reveal digits individually. Instead, it gives compact numeric feedback: direction, proximity, and final confirmation. That keeps the puzzle quick while still rewarding careful interval narrowing.

  • Too low - the target is greater than your guess.
  • Too high - the target is smaller than your guess.
  • Close - your guess is within 1,000 of the answer.
  • Hit - exact match, puzzle complete.

NUMBER GUESSING GAME GUIDE

Make every guess count.

Guessing a number is more interesting when each attempt gives you useful information. RNGDLE turns a familiar higher-or-lower game into a compact daily puzzle. You are not trying to be lucky; you are trying to reduce the possible interval before your six guesses run out.

On the daily five-digit board, the answer is between 00001 and 99999. A first guess near the center quickly tells you which half to keep. After that, choose the midpoint of the remaining interval. When a guess is within 1,000, the close clue changes the job from broad search to precision. Keep the last few guesses close together and use the direction of each result to move deliberately.

Every daily puzzle is stable for the date, so you can play once, compare your result with friends, and come back when the next number unlocks. The game runs in your browser and stores your local attempts on this device. There is no account, timer pressure during play, or hidden requirement to buy anything.
